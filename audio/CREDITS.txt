Audio sources (all CC0 1.0 / public domain - no attribution required, credited anyway)

sea_shore.wav, sea_distant.wav
  "Sea and river wave sounds" by RandomMind
  https://opengameart.org/content/sea-and-river-wave-sounds
  Trimmed, loudness-normalised and made into seamless loops (sea_distant is low-passed).

birds.wav
  "Birds and Wind - Ambient" by Spring Spring
  (bird recordings by isaiah658, syncopika and pauliuw, all public domain)
  https://opengameart.org/content/birds-and-wind-ambient-birds-wind-and-synth
  Loudness-normalised and made into a seamless loop.

footsteps/soft_*.wav, footsteps/stone_*.wav
  "Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)" by Fantozzi, packaged by qubodup
  https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
  Leading silence trimmed, converted to mono, loudness-normalised.

wind.wav
  Synthesised for this project with ffmpeg (filtered brown/pink noise with gust envelopes).

footsteps/water_1-7.wav, footsteps/water_land_1-2.wav
  "40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX" by rubberduck (splash_02/03/06/08/12/14/15, splash_04/07)
  https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx
  Leading silence trimmed, shortened with fade-outs, converted to mono, loudness-normalised.

footsteps/water_land_3.wav
  "Water Splash and sand footsteps" by Peludo (splash2.wav)
  https://opengameart.org/content/water-splash-and-sand-footsteps
  Leading silence trimmed, shortened with a fade-out, loudness-normalised.
